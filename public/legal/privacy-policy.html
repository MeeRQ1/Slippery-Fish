1. Who we are

Slippery Fish (“the game”) is published by [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS] (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. The short version

  • The game runs entirely in your web browser. We do not run accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking in this version.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only in your own browser on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game’s fonts and art are bundled with the game, so playing does not contact third-party font, analytics or ad servers.
  • You can erase your data at any time (see section 6).

3. Information stored on your device

Game progress: levels, stars, best times, Infinite seed history, Daily Challenge progress, currencies, Hoods, Waddles, quests and gameplay statisticsYour browser’s IndexedDB storage (or localStorage, or memory only if your browser blocks storage)So your progress is still there next time you play

4. Information processed when the game is delivered

The game’s files are served by our website host, [HOSTING PROVIDER — e.g. GitHub Pages]. Like any website, the host’s servers receive technical information needed to deliver files, such as your IP address, browser user-agent and the time of the request, and may keep it in server logs for security and operations. We do not receive these logs. See the host’s own privacy statement: [HOST PRIVACY STATEMENT URL].

5. What we do not do (in this version)

  • No user accounts or sign-in, no cloud saves.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers. (The “Watch Ads” / rewarded-ad options are shown as unavailable.)
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, fingerprinting or third-party cookies.
  • No real-money purchases and no payment information.
  • No online multiplayer: Ranked online matches are unavailable in this version.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

If any of these features are added later (for example rewarded ads, accounts, cloud saves, online Ranked matches or purchases), this policy will be updated before they go live, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent first.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Erase in the game: Settings → Save & Account → “Erase This Device’s Save”.
  • Erase in your browser: clear this site’s data (cookies and site data / storage) in your browser settings.
  • Keep a copy: Settings → Export Save downloads your progress as a file you control.

Because we do not hold any of your game data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. See the Data Deletion & Privacy Requests page.
