Common questions

How do I play?

Move your penguin with W A S D (or the arrow keys) and hold Left Shift to sprint. On touch screens use the joystick and hold the sprint button. Bump the fish into the stash — they’re physical and slippery, so you dribble them, you can’t pick them up. Avoid polar bears, arctic wolves and seals: they eat fish!

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved in your browser only. It is lost if site data is cleared, if you play in a private window, or if you switch browser or device. Use Settings → Export Save to keep a backup, and Import Save to restore it.

Why can’t I watch ads or buy things?

Rewarded ads and real-money purchases are not connected in this version. Everything can be earned by playing.

Why is Ranked unavailable?

Online Ranked matches need a dedicated game server that this version does not have. You can still play an offline practice run under Ranked rules.

The game is slow.

Try Settings → Graphics → Quality: Low, close other tabs, and make sure hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser.
