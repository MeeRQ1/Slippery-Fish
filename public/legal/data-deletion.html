Is there an account to delete?

No. This version of Slippery Fish does not create online accounts and does not send your progress to any server. Everything the game saves is stored in your own browser on your own device.

How to delete your game data

  1. In the game: open Settings → Save & Account → “Erase This Device’s Save”, type ERASE and confirm. This permanently removes your progress from this browser.
  2. In your browser: open your browser’s settings and clear the site data (storage) for this website. This removes the save and your settings.

Each browser and device keeps its own separate save, so repeat these steps on every browser or device where you played.
